I am a final-year M.Sc. student in the [PALM Lab](https://palm.seu.edu.cn/) at [Southeast University (SEU)](https://www.seu.edu.cn/english/), advised by Prof. [Hongsong Wang](https://cs.seu.edu.cn/hongsongwang/main.htm), and expect to graduate in June 2027. I am currently a research intern at [Tencent HY](https://hunyuan.tencent.com/) and also collaborate remotely with Prof. [Ming-Hsuan Yang](https://faculty.ucmerced.edu/mhyang/) at [UC Merced](https://www.ucmerced.edu/). Previously, I interned at [Tencent's Youtu Lab](https://open.youtu.qq.com/#/open) and, before that, was a remote visiting student in the [LV LAB](https://www.lv-lab.org/SMU/index.html) at Singapore Management University (SMU), working closely with Prof. [Pan Zhou](https://panzhous.github.io/). I received dual bachelor's degrees in Computer Science (B.E.) and Mathematics (B.Sc.) from [Shenzhen University (SZU)](https://en.szu.edu.cn/) in 2024.

My research focuses on RL, AIGC, and world models, especially human-centric perception, generation, and understanding. I also serve as a reviewer for ICML (Gold Reviewer), NeurIPS (Top Reviewer), AAAI, ICLR, TMLR, and IJDSA.

I am looking for **Ph.D.** opportunities starting in Fall 2027.
